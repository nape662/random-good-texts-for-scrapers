# Сплин — «Моё сердце»: вольный перевод

Хороший вольный перевод на английский под оригинальную мелодию, с пропуском двух куплетов.

[Оригинальный текст песни](https://genius.com/Splean-my-heart-lyrics)

## English adaptation

We had never met, never before that September  
We were roaming the planet by land and by sea  
Then we booked, quite by chance that one flight I remember  
And the seat next to yours was the one meant for me  

And maybe you’ve never tasted that Hollywood glory  
You don’t walk down the catwalk in your lingerie  
No one’s chasing you down for a tabloid love story  
And you sing a bit softer than Montserrat Caballé  
Well, thank God, I’m no Brad Pitt, no worldwide sensation,  
Never up for an Oscar, never scored against France  
My name never made it to a street or a station  
But the curtains are closed and the rest is romance  

And my own heart, it came to a standstill  
And my own heart, it froze in time  

And my own heart, it came to a standstill  
It just paused for a moment  
Then beat as before
