# Сплин — «Моё сердце»: вольный перевод

Хороший вольный перевод на английский под оригинальную мелодию, с пропуском двух куплетов.

## English adaptation

We had never met, never before that September  
We were roaming the planet by land and by sea  
Then we booked, quite by chance that one flight I remember  
And the seat next to yours was the one meant for me 


And my own heart, it came to a standstill  
And my own heart, it froze in time  

And maybe you’ve never tasted that Hollywood glory  
You don’t walk down the catwalk in your lingerie  
No one’s chasing you down for a tabloid love story  
And you sing a bit softer than Montserrat Caballé  
Well, thank God, I’m no Brad Pitt, no worldwide sensation,  
Never up for an Oscar, never scored against France  
And my name never made it to a street or a station  
But the curtains are closed and the rest is romance.  

And my own heart, it came to a standstill  
And my own heart, it froze in time  

And my own heart, it came to a standstill  
It just paused for a moment  
Then beat as before

# Милая моя (Визбор)

## English

Милая моя / Darling, Love of Mine

All of our meetings must end, it's a law old as time,
Quiet and sad runs the stream by the old amber pine.
Embers grow dim under ash that is timid and gray,
Now it is over — it's time to be going away.

Darling, love of mine, forest sun that greets me —
Where, in what far land will you come to meet me?

Folded, the wings of the tents — now their flying is done,
Seeker of partings, the plane, wings unfurled to the sun.
Shyly the gangway is backing away from the wing,
Now an abyss lies between us: a literal thing.

Darling, love of mine, forest sun that greets me —
Where, in what far land will you come to meet me?

Keep all your comforting words — yes, I know that they're kind,
I'd only search for that stream by the old amber pine.
What if through mist there's a glimmer of red — could it be?
What if beside it — imagine! — they're waiting for me!

Darling, love of mine, forest sun that greets me —
Where, in what far land will you come to meet me?
